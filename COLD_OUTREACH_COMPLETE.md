## 🚨 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### 1. **Sending Too Many Emails Too Fast**
❌ **Bad**: Send 200 emails/day on Day 1
✅ **Good**: Start with 10 emails/day, increase 10%/day

### 2. **Generic Templates**
❌ **Bad**: "Dear Sir/Madam, I hope this email finds you well"
✅ **Good**: "Sarah, I noticed TechVentures is scaling your remote team..."

### 3. **No Follow-Up**
❌ **Bad**: Send Email 1, give up if no reply
✅ **Good**: 3-step sequence over 7 days (persistent but respectful)

### 4. **Weak CTAs**
❌ **Bad**: "Let me know if you're interested"
✅ **Good**: "Do you have 5 minutes this Thursday for a quick sandbox walkthrough?"

### 5. **Ignoring Deliverability**
❌ **Bad**: Use Gmail, no SPF/DKIM, send 500 emails/day
✅ **Good**: Custom domain, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, warm up gradually

## 🎓 Best Practices from Top Performers

### 1. **Research Before Sending** (2-3 minutes per lead)
- Check LinkedIn profile (recent posts, articles)
- Visit company website (recent blog posts, news)
- Check Crunchbase (recent funding, team size)
- Scan CEO/CTO Twitter (recent tweets about hiring)

### 2. **Personalize Subject Line** (increases open rate by 30%)
- Include company name: "{{company}}"
- Reference specific pain point: "Latency & payment friction"
- Avoid spam words: "Free", "Guaranteed", "Limited time"

### 3. **Keep Emails Short** (<150 words)
- 3-4 short paragraphs
- Bullet points for key benefits
- Clear single CTA (not multiple CTAs)
- White space for readability

### 4. **Test Everything** (A/B tests)
- Subject lines (2 variants)
- Opening lines (2 variants)
- CTAs (2 variants)
- Send times (morning vs. afternoon)

### 5. **Follow Up Persistently** (but respectfully)
- 3 emails over 7 days (not 1 email and give up)
- Reference previous email: "Re: Eliminating friction"
- Provide new value in each email (not just "bump")
- End with breakup email: "Last note from me"
