## 💰 Revenue Breakdown (Example: $10k Milestone)

### Input
- **Milestone Value**: $10,000 USD
- **Currency Conversion**: USD → NGN
- **Market FX Rate**: 1 USD = 1,560 NGN

### Step-by-Step Calculation

#### 1. Platform Fee Capture (15%)
```
Platform Fee = $10,000 × 15% = $1,500
Net to Talent = $10,000 - $1,500 = $8,500
```

#### 2. FX Conversion with Spread (0.75%)
```
Market Rate:      1 USD = 1,560 NGN
VETTED Rate:      1 USD = 1,548.3 NGN (0.75% markup)
Talent Receives:  $8,500 × 1,548.3 = 13,160,550 NGN

FX Spread Profit:
Market Value:     $8,500 × 1,560 = 13,260,000 NGN
VETTED Pays:      13,160,550 NGN
Difference:       99,450 NGN = $63.75 USD
```

#### 3. Total Revenue
```
Platform Fee:  $1,500.00
FX Spread:     $   63.75
─────────────────────────
TOTAL REVENUE: $1,563.75 (15.64% margin)
```
