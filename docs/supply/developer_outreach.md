## 🚨 Common Objections & Responses

### Objection 1: "Is this another Upwork/Toptal?"

**Response**:
> "No. Upwork/Toptal are marketplaces that take 20% fees and introduce 3-5 day payment delays. VettedME is trust infrastructure—think of it like a passport. We charge 15% (vs. 20%), provide instant escrow security (VettedPay), and settle payments in <24hrs with zero wire fees."

---

### Objection 2: "Why do I need biometric verification?"

**Response**:
> "Biometric verification (Smile ID/Persona face matching) prevents identity fraud and account sharing. Your VettedME Passport is cryptographically bound to your real-world identity. This is what allows us to offer instant payment releases—clients trust that the person completing the work is the verified passport holder."

---

### Objection 3: "How much does it cost?"

**Response**:
> "Creating your VettedME Passport is **free**. We only charge when you earn: 15% platform fee on each milestone payment. This covers escrow security (VettedPay), biometric verification (Smile ID/Persona), and compliance (automated W-8BEN tax forms). Zero monthly fees, zero listing fees."

---

### Objection 4: "What if clients don't pay?"

**Response**:
> "Impossible. VettedPay **locks client funds in escrow before you start work**. The moment you complete a milestone, pass client review, and verify your face scan, payment auto-releases to your bank account. Clients can't withdraw funds once escrowed. This is programmatic—not manual."
