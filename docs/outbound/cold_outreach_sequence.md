# 🎯 VETTED B2B Cold Email Outreach Sequence

## Strategic Overview

This 3-step email sequence is designed for **US and European engineering leaders** (CTOs, VPs of Engineering, Technical Founders) who are scaling distributed engineering teams across Africa and Latin America.

**Key Positioning**: VETTED is **NOT** a recruiting agency. VETTED is a **trust and transaction infrastructure platform** that eliminates offshore engineering friction through biometric identity verification and programmatic escrow settlement.

## 💡 Best Practices & Pro Tips

### Email Deliverability

1. **Domain Setup** (CRITICAL):
   - Use custom domain: `yourname@vettedforce.com` (NOT Gmail/Outlook)
   - Configure SPF, DKIM, DMARC records
   - Warm up domain gradually (start with 10 emails/day, increase 10%/day)

2. **Email Validation**:
   - Use NeverBounce or ZeroBounce (validate before sending)
   - Remove catch-all domains (risky bounces)
   - Remove role-based emails (info@, support@, etc.)

3. **Sending Volume**:
   - Start with 50 emails/day (1st month)
   - Increase to 100 emails/day (2nd month)
   - Max 200 emails/day (3rd month+)
   - Use multiple sending domains if sending >200/day

### Personalization Best Practices

1. **Do**:
   - Research each lead for 2-3 minutes (LinkedIn, company website, recent tweets)
   - Reference specific trigger events (funding, hiring, blog posts)
   - Use first name in opening line
   - Mention specific corridor country (Lagos, Nairobi, São Paulo)
   - Include company name in subject line

2. **Don't**:
   - Use generic templates ("Dear Sir/Madam")
   - Over-personalize (creepy stalker vibes)
   - Send immediately after connecting on LinkedIn (wait 24-48 hours)
   - Use attachments (gets flagged as spam)
   - Send on weekends (lower open rates)

### Timing Optimization

**Best Days to Send**:
- **Tuesday**: 10am-12pm EST (highest open rates)
- **Wednesday**: 2pm-4pm EST (highest reply rates)
- **Thursday**: 10am-12pm EST (second-best open rates)

**Worst Days to Send**:
- **Monday**: Too busy, inbox overload
- **Friday**: Planning weekend, low engagement
- **Weekends**: Professional emails ignored

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. **Positioning as Recruiting Agency**
❌ **Bad**: "We provide top African developers"
✅ **Good**: "We built trust infrastructure for global engineering teams"

### 2. **Generic Subject Lines**
❌ **Bad**: "Hire great developers"
✅ **Good**: "Eliminating offshore engineering friction at {{company}}"

### 3. **Too Long Emails**
❌ **Bad**: 300+ word emails with multiple paragraphs
✅ **Good**: <150 words, 3-4 short paragraphs

### 4. **No Clear CTA**
❌ **Bad**: "Let me know if you're interested"
✅ **Good**: "Do you have 5 minutes this Thursday for a quick sandbox walkthrough?"

### 5. **Spam Trigger Words**
❌ **Avoid**: "Free", "Guaranteed", "Limited time", "Act now", "Click here"
✅ **Use**: "Infrastructure", "Protocol", "Automated", "Programmatic"
